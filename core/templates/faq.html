1 Tournament Data & Coverage #

Is the Magic Online Challenge data incomplete? #

Daybreak Games (the studio operating MTGO) typically publishes only the top 32 decks from Magic Online Challenges, regardless of total tournament players. Whether a Challenge draws 64, 128, or 250+ players, only the top 32 decklists and standings are published in the official MTGO tournament results dumps.

Because finishes below 32nd place are omitted from official results dumps, our Challenge Share and metagame metrics reflect only these top 32 lists. Decks that finish outside the top 32 are effectively invisible to ModoMeta. Some analyses like the Legacy Data Collection Project attempt to capture a more complete picture with painstaking work (support them!), but ModoMeta only uses the official published results.

What about Preliminaries and Last Chance Qualifiers (LCQs)? #

Preliminaries (usually 4-round events) and Last Chance Qualifiers (LCQs) are largely excluded from ModoMeta's metagame statistics because there are relatively few of them in the dataset. While we ingest these events for tournament and player records, they are excluded from Challenge and League share metrics.

How do League 5-0 decks work? #

Daybreak publishes decks that achieved a 5-0 match record in constructed Leagues. Our League Share metric counts the number of 5-0 decks published for each archetype over the total 5-0 league decks. However, decks that don't achieve a 5-0 are not published, so we don't have visibility into the total number of league decks played.

2 Metagame Metrics Explained #

Challenge Share #

The percentage of all published Challenge decks (typically 32 per event) represented by a given archetype within the selected timeframe. Reflects competitive adoption at the premier tournament level.

Top 8 Share #

The percentage of total available Top 8 playoff spots (8 × challenge_count) captured by this archetype. High Top 8 Share indicates high absolute tournament success.

Conversion Rate #

Calculated as Top 8 Finishes / Challenge Appearances. Measures an archetype's win efficiency: when a player brings this deck, how likely are they to convert their run into a Top 8 appearance? Very strong decks, especially those with lower overall play rates, will have a high conversion rate.

League Share #

The proportion of all verified League 5-0 trophies earned by an archetype within the selected timeframe. While Challenges represent premier competitive play, League Share reflects everyday popularity, accessibility, and brewing viability.

3 Classification & Color Inferences #

How does ModoMeta identify archetypes and colors? #

Decklists are processed through a multi-stage classification pipeline:

  1. Curated Signatures: Formats define archetype signatures with mandatory cards, minimum signature card thresholds, and anti-signatures (cards that exclude a deck from an archetype, like preventing Delver lists with Bowmasters from matching Izzet Delver).
  2. TF-IDF & k-Nearest Neighbors (kNN): Unclassified decks are matched against known archetypes using TF-IDF cosine similarity with recency decay.
  3. True Color Deductions: Color identities are inferred by examining whether the deck's mana base (lands and permanent mana producers) can produce the colors required by its castable spells. Free pitch spells/cards (Faerie Macabre) and Phyrexian mana (Dismember, Surgical Extraction) do not count toward a deck's color identity unless its mana base actually produces their colors.
